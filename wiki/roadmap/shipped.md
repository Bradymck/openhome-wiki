### what-it-is
The roadmap encompasses features or milestones that outline the development status and future directions of the project, detailing what has been shipped, what is in progress, and what is planned.

### status
- Shipped: Features or milestones that have been fully implemented and are operational.
- In-progress: Features or milestones currently being developed or tested.
- Planned: Features or milestones that are intended for future development.
- Cancelled: Features or milestones that have been abandoned or will no longer be pursued.

### why-it-matters
The roadmap is crucial for stakeholders to understand the project’s direction, manage expectations, and align development efforts with user needs and market demands. It serves as a communication tool that enhances transparency and promotes accountability within the development team and to external users.

### eta
A rough timeline is typically provided for planned or in-progress features to keep users informed of expected delivery dates. Specific ETAs may vary by feature.
