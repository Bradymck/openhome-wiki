## what-it-is
The platform marketplace is an online space designed for users to discover, purchase, and utilize various digital products and services that enhance the functionality and experience of the OpenHome ecosystem.

## how-it-works
The marketplace functions as a digital storefront for developers and creators to list their offerings, which can include software, applications, themes, and other enhancements tailored for the OpenHome platform. Users can browse through these offerings, read descriptions, view ratings, and make purchases directly within the marketplace interface. Integration with the OpenHome platform allows for seamless installation and management of purchased items.
