## what-it-is
The Live Editor is a component of the OpenHome platform that allows users to create, edit, and manage content in real-time, providing an interactive editing experience.

## how-it-works
The Live Editor integrates seamlessly into the OpenHome platform, enabling users to make changes to their content and see those changes reflected immediately without the need for reloading or refreshing the interface. This functionality enhances the user experience by offering dynamic content creation and editing capabilities.
