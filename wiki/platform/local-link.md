## what-it-is
Platform Local-Link is a feature of the OpenHome ecosystem that facilitates local network connectivity among devices, enabling seamless interaction and communication within a home environment.

## how-it-works
Local-Link operates by creating a direct connection between compatible devices on the same local network, allowing them to exchange information and control commands efficiently. This reduces reliance on external cloud services and enhances the responsiveness of device interactions, providing users with a more integrated and user-friendly experience.
