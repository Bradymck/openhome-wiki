## what-it-is
The Local Link is a component of the OpenHome platform that facilitates communication and integration between local devices and the broader OpenHome ecosystem.

## how-it-works
Local Link operates by enabling devices within the same local network to discover and connect to each other seamlessly. This functionality ensures that users can control and interact with their devices without relying on cloud infrastructure, reducing latency and increasing responsiveness.
