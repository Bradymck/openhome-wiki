## what-it-is
The Local Link is a component of the OpenHome platform that facilitates communication and interaction between local devices and services within a network.

## how-it-works
Local Link operates by enabling devices within a designated local network to discover and connect with each other seamlessly, allowing for interactive functionalities and resource sharing. This is essential for creating a cohesive home automation environment where various OpenHome-compatible devices can work together effectively.
