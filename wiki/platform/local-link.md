### what-it-is
The local-link platform is a feature within the OpenHome ecosystem that facilitates connections between local devices to enhance seamless interactions and functionalities within local networks.

### how-it-works
The local-link feature operates by allowing devices connected to the same local network to communicate with each other, sharing data and functionality. This enhances user experiences by enabling faster responses and reducing latency typically associated with cloud-based services. Local-link leverages protocols designed for low-latency, high-efficiency data transfer, making it easier for users to control their devices in real-time.
