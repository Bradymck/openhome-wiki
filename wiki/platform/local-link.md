### what-it-is
Local Link is a functionality within the OpenHome platform that facilitates device connectivity and communication over a local network, enhancing the user experience in home automation and multimedia sharing.

### how-it-works
Local Link operates by establishing a seamless connection among compatible devices within the same local network. This allows for easy discovery and control of devices, enabling users to manage their home systems efficiently. By utilizing protocols designed for low-latency communication, Local Link ensures that commands are executed promptly, providing real-time feedback to users.
