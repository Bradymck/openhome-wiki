## what-it-is
Voice AI is a technology designed to enable devices to understand and respond to human voice commands, facilitating seamless interaction between users and their devices within the OpenHome ecosystem.

## how-it-works
Voice AI operates by utilizing advanced speech recognition and natural language processing algorithms to interpret spoken language. This involves capturing audio input from users, converting it into text, and then analyzing the text to determine the appropriate response or action. The system is integrated with various OpenHome devices, allowing for a hands-free experience in controlling smart environments, retrieving information, and executing commands.
