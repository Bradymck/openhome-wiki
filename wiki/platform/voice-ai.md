## what-it-is
Voice AI is a component of the OpenHome platform that enables devices to understand and process spoken commands, allowing for hands-free control and interaction with various services.

## how-it-works
Voice AI utilizes natural language processing (NLP) algorithms to interpret user speech. When a user speaks a command or query, the system converts the audio input into text, processes the instruction through its AI models, and generates an appropriate response or action. This enables seamless interaction between users and the OpenHome ecosystem, facilitating tasks such as controlling smart devices, accessing information, and executing skills seamlessly.
