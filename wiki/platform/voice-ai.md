## what-it-is
Voice AI refers to the integration of artificial intelligence technologies within a platform that enables voice recognition and voice command functionalities, enhancing user interactions with devices.

## how-it-works
Voice AI typically utilizes natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms to interpret and respond to user voice commands. This allows users to control devices, access information, and perform tasks simply through spoken words. The interaction is facilitated by microphones that capture audio input, which is then processed in real-time or through cloud services to understand user intent and generate appropriate responses.
