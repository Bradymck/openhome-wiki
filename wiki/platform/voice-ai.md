## what-it-is
Voice AI is a technology that enables devices to understand and interpret spoken commands, allowing users to interact with various applications and services through natural language.

## how-it-works
Voice AI systems utilize a combination of natural language processing (NLP), machine learning, and speech recognition technologies to convert spoken language into text, understand its meaning, and respond accordingly. Users typically initiate interaction by speaking a command or query, which the system processes to return a relevant response or action. This technology can be integrated into various platforms and devices, enhancing user experience and accessibility.
