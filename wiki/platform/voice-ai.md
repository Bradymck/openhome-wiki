## what-it-is
Voice AI refers to the technology that enables devices and applications to understand and respond to human voice commands, facilitating hands-free interaction and automation of tasks.

## how-it-works
Voice AI systems typically leverage a combination of natural language processing (NLP), machine learning, and speech recognition technologies. These systems process audio input from users, convert speech into text, analyze the text to determine intent, and then execute commands or respond with appropriate information. This allows users to interact with devices in a more conversational manner, making technology more accessible and intuitive.
