## what-it-is
Voice AI is an artificial intelligence technology that allows users to interact with devices and services through speech recognition and natural language processing. It enables voice-based commands and conversational interfaces, streamlining user interactions across various platforms.

## how-it-works
Voice AI systems utilize advanced algorithms to interpret spoken language, enabling them to understand and respond to user inquiries. These systems convert voice input into text, process the meaning using natural language processing techniques, and generate appropriate responses or actions. Voice AI can be integrated into applications and devices, enhancing the user experience by providing hands-free control and accessibility.
