## what-it-is
OpenHome is a platform that facilitates the creation and integration of connected home devices, allowing users to control and manage various smart home functionalities through a cohesive interface.

## how-it-works
OpenHome works by enabling interoperability between different smart devices and systems through a unified protocol. It provides a framework that developers can use to build applications that interact with home automation products, such as speakers, lights, and other smart appliances. The platform's architecture emphasizes ease of use and accessibility, aiming to deliver seamless experiences for both end-users and developers.
