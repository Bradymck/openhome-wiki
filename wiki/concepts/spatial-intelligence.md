## definition
Spatial intelligence refers to the ability to visualize and manipulate objects in a given space. It encompasses skills such as spatial reasoning, understanding geometric relationships, and navigating through environments.

## why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, spatial intelligence is crucial as it informs how users interact with their virtual and physical spaces. Enhancing spatial intelligence can lead to more intuitive interfaces and interactions within the platform, improving user experience and accessibility.

## how-it-manifests
Spatial intelligence can manifest in various ways across the OpenHome platform, particularly in features related to layout design, 3D modeling, and augmented reality experiences. These functionalities empower users to better understand and utilize space within their homes or digital products.
