## definition
Spatial intelligence is the capacity to think in three dimensions, allowing individuals to visualize and manipulate objects in space. This type of intelligence involves the ability to recognize patterns and navigate environments effectively.

## why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, spatial intelligence is critical for creating intuitive user experiences and interactions within virtual spaces. It enhances how users relate to their surroundings, making navigation and interaction smoother and more engaging.

## how-it-manifests
This concept appears in OpenHome through features that leverage spatial awareness, such as augmented reality functionalities, 3D modeling, and spatial audio processing. These features enable users to interact with virtual environments that mimic real-world spatial dynamics, improving usability and immersion.
