### definition
Spatial intelligence refers to the capability to perceive, think, and reason about spatial relationships in the real world. It encompasses the ability to visualize and manipulate spatial dimensions and allows individuals to navigate and interact with their environment effectively.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, spatial intelligence is crucial as it enhances user interaction with the platform, making it more intuitive and user-friendly. It enables a better understanding of the spatial context users are in, which can improve their overall experience and engagement with the features offered by OpenHome.

### how-it-manifests
Spatial intelligence can manifest in various ways within OpenHome, including features that allow users to map their environments, visualize data in a spatial context, and interact with virtual or augmented reality applications. It also plays a role in optimizing the layout of physical spaces to facilitate smooth interactions among users and devices.
