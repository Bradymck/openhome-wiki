### definition
Spatial intelligence refers to the ability to visualize and manipulate spatial relationships and understand the dimensions and layouts of physical spaces. This concept involves recognizing and interpreting spatial configurations and understanding how objects relate to each other in a given environment.

### why-it-matters
Spatial intelligence is crucial for creating intuitive user experiences within OpenHome. It enables users to interact with their home environments in a more natural and efficient way, utilizing the spatial context to enhance functionality and accessibility.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, spatial intelligence might be applied in features such as augmented reality interfaces, where users can visualize home layouts or placements of furniture and appliances. It can enhance location-based services that allow users to interact with their environment based on its spatial characteristics.
