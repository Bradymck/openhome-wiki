### definition
Web3 Native refers to applications and platforms that are built on decentralized web technologies, utilizing principles of blockchain, decentralized governance, and user-centric models. This paradigm focuses on giving individuals ownership and control over their data, identity, and online interactions.

### why-it-matters
Web3 Native technologies are significant for OpenHome as they align with the platform’s goals of decentralization and user empowerment. By leveraging Web3 principles, OpenHome aims to create an ecosystem where users have greater control over their digital assets and interactions, fostering trust and transparency.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, Web3 Native concepts can be seen in features like decentralized identity management, which allows users to interact without relying on centralized authorities. Additional aspects may include token-based incentives for community participation and governance structures that enable users to have a say in decision-making processes.
