## definition
Web3-native refers to applications and services built on decentralized web technologies that prioritize user autonomy, privacy, and security. This paradigm shift takes advantage of blockchain and decentralized networks to provide users with control over their digital identities and assets.

## why-it-matters
Web3-native applications are significant for OpenHome as they align with the platform's goals of decentralization and user empowerment. By leveraging these technologies, OpenHome fosters a community-driven environment where users can fully own and manage their data, thereby enhancing trust and engagement on the platform.

## how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, the principles of web3-native technologies manifest through features such as decentralized identity management, user-controlled data storage, and transparent governance mechanisms. These features ensure that users have a direct stake in the platform's ecosystem, promoting a collaborative and secure experience.
