### definition
Web3-native refers to applications and services that are designed to operate on decentralized networks, prioritizing user ownership, privacy, and interoperability. This concept is fundamental to the evolution of the internet, enabling users to interact and transact in a trustless environment.

### why-it-matters
Web3-native technologies represent a shift from traditional centralized systems to decentralized ecosystems, where users have more control over their data and interactions. In the context of OpenHome, embracing web3-native principles can enhance user experience, increase trust, and foster a more resilient platform where users can collaboratively build and interact without reliance on central authorities.

### how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, web3-native concepts may manifest through decentralized applications (dApps) built on blockchain technology, allowing for peer-to-peer interactions, secure transactions, and user governance. Features such as tokenomics, where users can earn tokens for contributions, and identity management systems that utilize decentralized identifiers (DIDs) also reflect the web3-native approach.
