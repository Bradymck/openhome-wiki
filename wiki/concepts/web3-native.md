Web3 native refers to technologies, applications, and platforms that are built on decentralized web protocols and blockchain technology. These innovations emphasize user control, data ownership, and trustless interactions.

Web3 native concepts are crucial for OpenHome as they align with the vision of a decentralized and user-centric internet. This ensures users have full control over their data while promoting transparency and security.

## how-it-manifests
