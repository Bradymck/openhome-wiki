## definition
Web3 native refers to applications, services, and platforms that are built using decentralized technologies and principles, leveraging blockchain and other distributed networks for enhanced user control and transparency.

## why-it-matters
Web3 native concepts are crucial for OpenHome as they align with the platform's goals of decentralization, user empowerment, and data sovereignty. They support the foundation of a more open and user-centric internet, which directly resonates with OpenHome's vision.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, web3 native features may manifest through decentralized identity systems, user-controlled data ownership, and community-driven governance mechanisms, allowing users to actively participate in decision-making processes and maintain control over their digital interactions.
