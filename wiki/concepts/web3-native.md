### definition
Web3 Native refers to applications, services, and systems built on decentralized networks, leveraging technologies such as blockchain to empower users through ownership, transparency, and decentralized governance.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, being Web3 Native emphasizes a shift towards user-centered design, where individuals have greater control over their digital assets, identities, and data. This approach aligns with the ethos of decentralization and enhances users' trust as they navigate digital environments.

### how-it-manifests
The Web3 Native concept manifests on the OpenHome platform through features that incorporate decentralized identity management, token-based systems for community engagement, and smart contracts that facilitate transparent and secure transactions between users. It underlines the integration of advanced cryptographic protocols and peer-to-peer interactions to create a user-centric ecosystem.
