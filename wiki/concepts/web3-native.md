## definition
Web3 native refers to applications, platforms, and technologies built on decentralized networks that leverage blockchain and cryptographic protocols to empower users with greater control over their data and interactions.

## why-it-matters
The concept of being web3 native is crucial to OpenHome as it aligns with the platform's vision of decentralization and user sovereignty. It emphasizes the importance of creating a user-centric environment where individuals have ownership and agency over their digital identities and assets.

## how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, the web3 native philosophy is reflected in features that promote decentralized identity management, peer-to-peer interactions, and the integration of smart contracts. These capabilities ensure that users can engage without relying on centralized authorities, thereby enhancing privacy, security, and user empowerment.
