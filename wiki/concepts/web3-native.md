### definition
Web3-native refers to applications, services, and platforms that are fundamentally built upon decentralized technologies, such as blockchain. These applications leverage systems that empower users through enhanced ownership, privacy, and control over their data.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, adopting web3-native principles enables a more user-centric platform. It aligns with the vision of decentralization, ensuring that users have control over their personal data and interactions. This shift also enhances security and trust among users, fostering a more resilient community.

### how-it-manifests
Web3-native elements can be found in OpenHome through features that prioritize user sovereignty, such as identity management systems that utilize decentralized identifiers (DIDs) and data privacy tools rooted in blockchain technologies. The platform might also support decentralized finance (DeFi) mechanisms for transactions, allowing users to interact without traditional intermediaries.
