## definition
Web3-native refers to applications and systems that are built on decentralized protocols and technologies, leveraging blockchain and peer-to-peer networks. It embodies the principles of decentralization, user ownership, and data sovereignty which are central to the Web3 movement.

## why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, being web3-native is crucial as it aligns with the goal of providing users with more control over their data and digital identities. By adopting a web3-native approach, OpenHome can ensure that user interactions are secure, transparent, and not mediated by centralized entities. This enhances trust and fosters a more equitable digital ecosystem.

## how-it-manifests
The web3-native concept appears throughout the OpenHome platform in features such as smart contract integrations, user governance mechanisms, and decentralized identity solutions. These elements empower users by enabling them to manage their own data and engage with the platform in a way that respects privacy and ownership rights.
