### definition
Web3-native refers to applications and platforms that are built on decentralized technologies such as blockchain. This concept prioritizes user sovereignty, transparency, and protocols that allow for trustless interactions between users without reliance on central authorities.

### why-it-matters
Web3-native integrates the principles of decentralization and user empowerment into the OpenHome ecosystem. It enhances user control over data and interactions, fostering a community-oriented environment that is pivotal for innovative applications within OpenHome.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, web3-native characteristics may manifest through applications that utilize smart contracts, decentralized finance (DeFi) mechanisms, or other blockchain-based functionalities. Such implementations may offer enhanced privacy and autonomy for users, aligning with the overall goals of user agency and distributed sharing within the platform.
