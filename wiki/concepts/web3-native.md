## definition
Web3-native refers to applications, services, and ecosystems that are built using decentralized technologies and principles, particularly those associated with blockchain and peer-to-peer networks.

## why-it-matters
Web3-native concepts are essential for OpenHome as they promote user ownership, decentralization, and enhanced security. By incorporating these principles, OpenHome aims to ensure that users have more control over their data and interactions within the platform.

## how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, web3-native features may manifest through the use of smart contracts, decentralized identity solutions, and tokenization of assets and services. These elements allow users to manage their own data and interact with others without reliance on centralized entities.
