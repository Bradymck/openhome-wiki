## definition
Web3 Native refers to applications or platforms that are built on decentralized technologies, such as blockchain, and prioritize user ownership, security, and interoperability. These platforms often leverage smart contracts and decentralized protocols to create a more user-centric internet experience.

## why-it-matters
Web3 Native technologies are relevant to OpenHome as they align with the principles of decentralization, user empowerment, and enhanced privacy. By adopting Web3 concepts, OpenHome can foster a community-driven ecosystem that allows users to interact and transact in a secure environment, promoting trust and collaboration among participants.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, Web3 Native principles manifest through the implementation of decentralized features, such as user-controlled data management and identity verification mechanisms that do not rely on central authorities. Additionally, smart contracts may be utilized to automate transactions and agreements within the ecosystem, ensuring transparency and security.
