## definition
Web3 native refers to applications and services designed specifically for the decentralized web (Web3), leveraging blockchain technology, smart contracts, and peer-to-peer networks. This approach emphasizes user control, transparency, and community engagement.

## why-it-matters
The web3 native concept is crucial to OpenHome as it aligns with the platform's goals of empowering users through decentralized technologies. By fostering an environment where users have full control over their data and interactions, OpenHome helps to create more secure and user-centric experiences.

## how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, web3 native principles manifest through features such as decentralized identity management, smart contract-driven interactions, and community governance mechanisms. These aspects enable users to engage with the platform in a way that promotes autonomy and collaboration, enhancing trust and ownership.
