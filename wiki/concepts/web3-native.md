## definition
Web3 Native refers to applications, services, and ecosystems built on decentralized protocols and technologies that enable peer-to-peer interactions without centralized intermediaries. It encompasses concepts related to blockchain, decentralized finance (DeFi), and user-owned data.

## why-it-matters
Web3 Native technologies are relevant to OpenHome as they empower users with more control over their data and interactions, promoting transparency and trust through decentralization. This approach aligns with OpenHome's commitment to user autonomy and privacy.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, Web3 Native principles are reflected in features that allow users to own their data, interact with decentralized applications (dApps), and participate in community governance through tokens or blockchain-based systems.
