## definition
Web3 native refers to applications and services that are built on decentralized networks, utilizing blockchain technology to enable direct user interaction without intermediaries. These platforms aim to empower users by giving them control over their data and digital assets, fostering a trustless ecosystem.

## why-it-matters
Web3 native concepts are integral to OpenHome as they align with the platform's mission of decentralization and user empowerment. By leveraging blockchain technology, OpenHome enhances user autonomy and privacy, allowing users to manage their own identities and data securely and transparently.

## how-it-manifests
The web3 native concept can be seen in OpenHome through its decentralized identity management systems, smart contracts for governing interactions, and the incorporation of decentralized finance (DeFi) elements. This approach not only serves to enhance user experience but also ensures that users retain ownership of their digital assets.
