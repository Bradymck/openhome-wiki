## definition
Web3 native refers to technologies and applications that are built to operate within the decentralized web ecosystem, leveraging blockchain and related technologies to provide a user-centric, trustless environment while prioritizing user ownership and control over data and assets.

## why-it-matters
The relevance of Web3 native concepts to OpenHome lies in their alignment with the platform's goals of decentralization, privacy, and user empowerment. By incorporating Web3 principles, OpenHome can enhance user experiences through enhanced security, ownership, and autonomy over personal and shared data.

## how-it-manifests
On the OpenHome platform, Web3 native elements may present in various features, such as decentralized identity systems, blockchain-enabled asset management, and user-controlled data sharing. These aspects ensure that users have full control over their digital assets and identities without relying on centralized authorities.
