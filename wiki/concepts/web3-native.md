## definition
Web3 Native refers to applications and platforms that are inherently built on decentralized web technologies, characterized by the use of blockchain, smart contracts, peer-to-peer networks, and decentralized identity systems. This concept emphasizes user ownership and control over their data and digital experiences, moving away from centralized models.

## why-it-matters
Web3 Native technologies are integral to OpenHome as they promote principles of decentralization and user agency. By empowering users to maintain control over their data and privacy, OpenHome aligns with the vision of a more open and equitable internet. This approach can lead to enhanced trust and security for users, making the platform more attractive.

## how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, the Web3 Native concept manifests through functionalities that allow users to interact without relying on central authorities. This can include decentralized authentication methods, rights management for user-generated content, and token-based economies that reward user participation and contribution. These elements create a seamless integration of decentralized technology within the everyday experiences of users on the platform.
