## definition
Web3-native refers to applications, services, or platforms that are built from the ground up using the technologies and principles associated with Web3, including blockchain, decentralized networks, and user empowerment.

## why-it-matters
Web3-native concepts are crucial for OpenHome as they embody the shift towards decentralization, enhancing user privacy, security, and ownership of data. They enable users to have more control over their digital identities and interactions.

## how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, web3-native technologies may appear in features that facilitate peer-to-peer interactions, smart contracts for automated agreements, and decentralized storage systems for user data. These implementations enable a more transparent and fair ecosystem for users.
