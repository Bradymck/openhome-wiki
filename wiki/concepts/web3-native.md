### definition
Web3-native refers to applications, services, and technologies built specifically for the decentralized web, utilizing blockchain technologies and protocols to empower user control, privacy, and autonomy.

### why-it-matters
Web3-native concepts are crucial to OpenHome as they promote a shift from traditional centralized models to decentralized approaches that prioritize user ownership of data and identity. This aligns with the mission of OpenHome to create an ecosystem that empowers individuals by leveraging innovative technologies that enhance privacy and user agency.

### how-it-manifests
The concept of web3-native can be seen in OpenHome's integration with blockchain technologies, allowing users to have greater control over their data, identity, and interactions within the platform. Decentralized applications (dApps) that operate within the OpenHome framework exemplify how this concept is applied, offering services that are resilient, transparent, and user-centric.
