### definition
Web3 native refers to applications and platforms that are built on decentralized protocols and technologies, enabling user ownership and control of their data and digital assets in the context of a distributed web.

### why-it-matters
In OpenHome, web3 native concepts are crucial for ensuring that users can interact with the platform in a manner that prioritizes privacy, security, and user autonomy. It aligns with the overarching vision of creating an ecosystem where individuals have more agency over their digital interactions.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, web3 native principles may be reflected through the integration of decentralized identity solutions, smart contracts, and user-governed protocols, allowing participants to have direct ownership over their contributions and data.
