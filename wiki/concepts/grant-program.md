## definition
A grant program is a structured initiative designed to provide funding to individuals, organizations, or projects that align with specific goals and criteria set by the granting entity.

## why-it-matters
Grant programs are crucial for fostering innovation, supporting community development, and encouraging the implementation of projects that contribute to the wider goals of OpenHome. They enable participants to access resources that might otherwise be unavailable, promoting diversified efforts and collaboration within the OpenHome ecosystem.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, grant programs may be manifested as funding opportunities for developers, startups, or community projects that seek to enhance the platform's accessibility, functionality, or user experience. This could include grants for developing new features, applications, or tools that integrate with OpenHome's philosophy and technological framework.
