## definition
A grant program refers to a financial support initiative aimed at providing funds to individuals, organizations, or communities to support projects that align with specific objectives or goals within the OpenHome ecosystem.

## why-it-matters
Grant programs are crucial for fostering innovation and collaboration within the OpenHome community. They enable participants to access resources needed to develop projects that enhance the platform, ultimately contributing to its growth and sustainability.

## how-it-manifests
The concept of grant programs can be seen in various initiatives organized by OpenHome, which invite proposals for funding in areas such as technology development, community engagement, and educational outreach. Projects funded through these grants typically align with the core values and objectives of the OpenHome platform.
