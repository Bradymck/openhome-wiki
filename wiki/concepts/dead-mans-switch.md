### definition
A dead man's switch is a safety mechanism that triggers an action when a certain condition is met, typically related to the absence of input or activity from the user. In digital platforms, it serves as a fail-safe, ensuring that if a user becomes incapacitated or unresponsive, certain pre-defined actions are executed automatically.

The dead man's switch is relevant to OpenHome as it enhances user security and reliability. By ensuring that users' preferences or digital assets are handled appropriately in scenarios where they can no longer engage with the platform, it promotes trust in the system's reliability and continuity.
