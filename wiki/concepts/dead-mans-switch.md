## definition
A dead man's switch is a safety mechanism that triggers an action when a particular person cannot initiate it themselves, often due to incapacitation or death. It can be a software feature that ensures certain operations are carried out or avoided under specific conditions.

## why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, the dead man's switch is relevant for ensuring the continuity and safety of automated systems, especially in scenarios where user dependency may present risks. It provides a fail-safe that protects users and systems from unintended lapses caused by inactivity.

## how-it-manifests
The concept of a dead man's switch may manifest within the OpenHome platform as a feature that automatically engages services or protocols if a user has not interacted with their system for a designated period. This could be especially pertinent in home automation and security contexts.
