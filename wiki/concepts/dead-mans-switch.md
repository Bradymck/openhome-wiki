### definition
A dead man's switch is a safety mechanism that automatically executes a predefined action when a user is unable to do so, often due to incapacitation or absence. It is commonly used in various systems to prevent adverse outcomes when a user fails to act.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, the dead man's switch is crucial for ensuring ongoing user control and security of their digital assets. It can help users manage their online presence and data in case of emergencies, thereby reinforcing trust within the platform.

### how-it-manifests
The concept of a dead man's switch may manifest in OpenHome as functionalities that allow users to set actions that occur if they do not log in or interact with their accounts over a certain period. This can include transferring ownership of assets, notifying contacts, or securing data.
