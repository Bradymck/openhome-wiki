### definition
A "Dead Man's Switch" refers to a safety mechanism that automatically triggers an action if a user becomes incapacitated or fails to respond. It is designed to ensure that a system continues to operate safely when the user cannot confirm their active involvement.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, a Dead Man's Switch can enhance user safety and system reliability by preventing unauthorized actions or ensuring continuity of essential functions even in the absence of user interaction. It acts as a safeguard for critical processes and operations.

### how-it-manifests
While specific implementations in OpenHome are not detailed, the concept can manifest through features that require periodic user confirmations, alerts for inaction, or automatic reversals of actions taken if the user does not affirmatively agree.
