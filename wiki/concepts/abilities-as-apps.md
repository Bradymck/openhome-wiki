### definition
Abilities-as-apps refer to the modularization of functionalities within a platform, allowing users to access and use specific capabilities as separate applications or features.

### why-it-matters
This concept is significant for OpenHome as it enhances user experience by enabling personalized access to various features. It facilitates user customization and flexibility within the platform, allowing users to select and engage with only the functionalities that meet their specific needs.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, abilities-as-apps appear as distinct applications or modules that users can deploy to extend the functionality of the system. This modular approach allows users to tailor their interaction with OpenHome based on their preferences and requirements.
