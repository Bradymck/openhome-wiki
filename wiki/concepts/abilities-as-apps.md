## definition
Abilities as Apps refers to the concept of modular functionalities or capabilities that can be treated and utilized as independent applications within the OpenHome ecosystem. This allows users to add, modify, or remove specific abilities based on their individual needs and preferences.

## why-it-matters
This concept is critical to OpenHome as it enhances user customization and flexibility. Users can tailor their experience by integrating various abilities that can function seamlessly together, improving overall satisfaction and engagement with the platform.

## how-it-manifests
Abilities as Apps manifests within the OpenHome platform through a library of available functionalities that users can easily access and implement. This structure promotes a dynamic interaction model, where users can experiment with different abilities, leading to a more personalized and innovative use of the system.
