### definition
The concept of "abilities-as-apps" refers to the idea that various functionalities or features can be treated as modular applications. This allows users to easily access, modify, and utilize different capabilities within a single platform, akin to how one would download and manage apps on a mobile device.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, abilities-as-apps is significant because it enhances user experience by providing flexibility and customization. This approach allows users to tailor the functionalities to their specific needs, promoting a more personalized interaction with the platform.

### how-it-manifests
This concept appears in the OpenHome platform through customizable interfaces where users can add or remove applications based on the abilities they wish to utilize. It also supports the integration of third-party apps, allowing a richer ecosystem of tools and functionalities.
