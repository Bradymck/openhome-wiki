## definition
Abilities-as-apps refers to the concept of modular functionalities that can be accessed as independent applications within the OpenHome platform. Each ability can perform a specific task or provide a distinct service, thereby enhancing user experience and customization.

## why-it-matters
This concept is crucial for OpenHome as it allows users to tailor their interactions based on individual needs and preferences. By implementing abilities as separate apps, the platform can foster a more diverse ecosystem where users can easily engage with various functionalities without being tied to a singular interface.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, abilities-as-apps are represented through a library of applications that users can select and activate. Each app operates independently yet integrates seamlessly within the overall system, making it possible for users to employ multiple abilities simultaneously, enhancing the interoperability of services.
