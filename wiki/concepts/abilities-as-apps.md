### definition
Abilities-as-apps refer to a framework in which various functional capabilities or features of a platform are packaged as standalone applications. This allows users to access specific functionalities independently without relying on a monolithic system.

This concept is significant to OpenHome as it promotes modular design, enhancing user flexibility and customization. By allowing users to select and integrate various abilities as needed, OpenHome facilitates a more personalized and user-centric experience, adapting to individual preferences and requirements.

### how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, abilities-as-apps are likely represented through a marketplace or library of applications that users can browse and install. Each app would provide distinct functionalities related to the overall system, enabling users to tailor their environment for efficiency and ease of use.
