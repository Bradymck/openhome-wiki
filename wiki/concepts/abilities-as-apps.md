## definition
Abilities-as-apps refers to the concept of encapsulating specific functionalities or capabilities within applications, allowing users to leverage various skills or tools independently within the OpenHome platform.

## why-it-matters
This concept enhances user experience by providing modular and customizable functionalities that can be independently activated or deactivated according to user needs. It emphasizes flexibility, allowing for tailored interactions with the platform without complexity.

## how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, abilities-as-apps appear as distinct applications that users can access to perform specific tasks, enhancing the functionality of the overall ecosystem. Users can mix and match these abilities to create a personalized interaction layer.
