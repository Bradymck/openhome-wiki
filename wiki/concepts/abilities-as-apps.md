## definition
Abilities-as-apps refers to a paradigm where individual capabilities or functionalities are encapsulated as distinct applications, allowing them to be easily accessed and integrated within a larger ecosystem.

## why-it-matters
This concept is crucial to the OpenHome platform as it facilitates modularity and flexibility. By enabling different abilities to function as standalone apps, users can customize their experiences according to their specific needs and preferences. This modular approach also encourages innovation, allowing developers to create and enhance individual apps without impacting the overall platform.

## how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, abilities-as-apps are implemented through various modules or extensions that users can enable or disable as desired. These abilities can range from simple functions, like basic home automation controls, to more complex interactions involving AI-driven tasks. The modular nature ensures that the platform evolves alongside the user’s changing requirements.
