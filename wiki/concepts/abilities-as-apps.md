### definition
Abilities-as-apps refer to the concept of treating functionalities or capabilities within a platform as discrete applications that users can access and utilize. This approach allows for modularity and flexibility in how users interact with technology.

### why-it-matters
This concept is significant to OpenHome as it enables users to customize their experiences and use only the functionalities they need. By breaking down abilities into app-like components, OpenHome can foster an environment that promotes user empowerment and adaptability.

### how-it-manifests
Within the OpenHome platform, abilities-as-apps may manifest as independent modules or widgets, allowing users to activate, deactivate, or switch between them based on their preferences. This modular approach enhances both usability and personalization, making the platform more accessible to a diverse range of users.
