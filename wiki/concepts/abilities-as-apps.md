## definition
Abilities-as-apps refer to the conceptual framework where specific functionalities or capabilities within a platform are represented as modular applications. This allows users to easily access and utilize distinct features seamlessly, enhancing user interaction.

## why-it-matters
This concept is important to OpenHome as it promotes flexibility and customization for users. By treating abilities as standalone applications, OpenHome can offer a more tailored experience, allowing users to select and configure the functionalities that best suit their needs.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, abilities-as-apps appear as independent modules that users can add, remove, or modify according to their preferences. This modular structure enables users to create a personalized environment that can adapt to different situations and requirements.
