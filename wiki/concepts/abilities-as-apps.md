### definition
Abilities-as-apps refer to the concept of modular functionalities within the OpenHome platform that can be added or integrated as discrete applications, providing specific capabilities and enhancing user experience.

### why-it-matters
This concept is significant within OpenHome as it allows for a customizable and adaptable user environment. By treating abilities as separate applications, users can select, modify, or remove functionalities according to their preferences, leading to a more personalized and efficient use of the platform.

### how-it-manifests
Abilities-as-apps are integrated into OpenHome's architecture, enabling users to access and utilize various functionalities that can operate independently or in conjunction with one another. The flexibility of this modular approach supports continuous improvement and innovation, allowing developers to create and deploy new abilities seamlessly.
