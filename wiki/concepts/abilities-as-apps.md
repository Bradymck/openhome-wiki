### definition
Abilities-as-apps refer to the concept of treating functionalities and services in the OpenHome ecosystem as discrete applications. This model allows for a modular approach where users can select and use specific abilities according to their needs.

### why-it-matters
This concept is crucial to OpenHome as it enhances user flexibility and customization, enabling individuals to tailor their experiences and interactions with the platform. It promotes a more user-centric design, allowing for the integration of various functionalities without requiring a monolithic approach.

### how-it-manifests
Abilities-as-apps can be found within the OpenHome platform where users can activate, configure, and manage different applications that correspond to specific functionalities. This modular system may include voice controls, automation tasks, or any personalized service that can be added or removed as per user preferences.
