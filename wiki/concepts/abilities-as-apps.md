### definition
Abilities-as-apps refers to the framework within OpenHome that allows various functionalities or capabilities to be encapsulated as individual applications. This approach simplifies the interaction between users and various services by treating abilities as standalone, modular apps.

### why-it-matters
This concept is important for OpenHome as it promotes flexibility and customization. Users can choose and utilize specific abilities according to their needs, enhancing the overall user experience. It supports a more user-friendly environment where functionalities can be easily accessed and integrated.

### how-it-manifests
The abilities-as-apps concept appears within the OpenHome platform as various modules or plug-ins that users can enable or configure. Each ability can be viewed as an app that provides specific features, such as managing devices, automating tasks, or interfacing with other services.
