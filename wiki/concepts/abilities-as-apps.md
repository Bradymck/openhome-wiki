## definition
Abilities-as-apps represent a modular approach to functionalities within the OpenHome platform, enabling individual skills and capabilities to be packaged as standalone applications. This concept empowers users to select and integrate specific functionalities that suit their needs, similar to how mobile apps are downloaded and used.

## why-it-matters
The relevance of abilities-as-apps to OpenHome lies in its flexibility and customization. By allowing users to choose from a variety of abilities, the platform can cater to diverse user preferences and requirements, promoting higher satisfaction and engagement. This approach also fosters innovation, as developers can create and deploy new abilities independently.

## how-it-manifests
This concept appears in the OpenHome platform through an app marketplace where users can browse, install, and manage different abilities. Each ability operates autonomously while seamlessly integrating with other abilities and the core functionalities of the platform, enhancing the overall user experience.
