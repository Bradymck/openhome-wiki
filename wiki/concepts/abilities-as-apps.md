### definition
The concept of "abilities-as-apps" refers to the modular approach of creating and integrating capabilities within the OpenHome platform, allowing users to access various functionalities as standalone applications.

### why-it-matters
This approach is significant because it enhances user customization and flexibility, enabling users to select and combine various abilities according to their needs without being locked into a singular system. It promotes a more dynamic and personalized user experience.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, abilities-as-apps can be found in various user interfaces and architectures, where different applications offer specific functionalities that can be easily integrated, activated, or modified by users, supporting a diverse ecosystem of tools and features.
