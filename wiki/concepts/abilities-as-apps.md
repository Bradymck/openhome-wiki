### definition
Abilities-as-apps refers to the concept of treating various functionalities or capabilities within a platform as if they were independent applications. This allows users to integrate, customize, and use these abilities in a modular fashion for a more personalized experience.

### why-it-matters
This concept is relevant to OpenHome as it enhances user interactivity and engagement. By allowing users to choose and combine different abilities as necessary, the platform can cater to diverse needs and preferences, encouraging wider adoption and better user satisfaction.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, abilities-as-apps may manifest as customizable modules that users can add or remove based on their requirements. Each ability can function independently or in conjunction with others, providing a flexible and user-friendly experience.
