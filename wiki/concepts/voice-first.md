## definition
Voice-first refers to an interaction paradigm wherein voice commands and responses are prioritized as the primary means for users to engage with technology. This approach leverages natural language processing and speech recognition to facilitate communication between users and devices.

## why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, voice-first technology is significant as it enhances user accessibility and engagement. By prioritizing voice interactions, OpenHome can cater to diverse user needs, making technology more intuitive and easier to use for a broader audience.

## how-it-manifests
Voice-first concepts appear in OpenHome through the integration of voice assistants and voice recognition systems within the platform, enabling users to control devices, access information, and perform functions using only their voice. This can include features like voice-activated commands for smart home devices and voice search capabilities.
