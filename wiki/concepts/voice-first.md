## definition
Voice-first refers to a design and interaction philosophy that prioritizes voice as the primary method for user interaction with technology. It emphasizes the use of voice commands and auditory feedback in enabling users to communicate with devices and applications, often leading to hands-free and eyes-free experiences.

## why-it-matters
Voice-first is relevant to OpenHome as it enhances accessibility and convenience for users, allowing them to engage with technology in a more natural manner. By prioritizing voice interactions, OpenHome is positioned to cater to diverse user needs, making its platform more inclusive and user-friendly.

## how-it-manifests
This concept appears in OpenHome through voice-activated features, allowing users to utilize speech to control home automation, access information, or manage applications. These functionalities are integrated into various aspects of the platform, supporting a seamless user experience that caters to those who prefer voice commands over traditional input methods.
