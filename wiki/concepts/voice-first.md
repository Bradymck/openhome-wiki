## definition
Voice-first refers to a design philosophy and technology approach that prioritizes voice interactions as the primary mode of engagement with digital devices and services. It emphasizes using voice commands and natural language processing to facilitate user interactions.

## why-it-matters
Voice-first is significant for OpenHome as it enables a more intuitive and accessible way for users to interact with the platform. By leveraging voice technologies, OpenHome can enhance user experience, streamline tasks, and make the platform more inclusive for individuals with varying abilities.

## how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, the voice-first concept manifests through features like voice commands for navigation, voice-activated services for content retrieval, and voice feedback for user actions. These capabilities aim to create a seamless and natural interaction environment for users.
