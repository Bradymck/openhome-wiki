## definition
Voice-first is a design philosophy that prioritizes voice interactions as the primary means of engaging with technology, rather than relying on traditional interfaces such as screens or keyboards. This approach emphasizes the use of spoken commands and conversations with devices.

## why-it-matters
Voice-first is relevant to OpenHome as it enhances user interaction by allowing for a more natural and intuitive way to engage with technology. This paradigm aligns with the increasing trend of users preferring verbal communication, making technology more accessible and user-friendly. It enables a shift towards a more seamless and integrated experience within digital environments.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, voice-first may manifest through voice-based commands that allow users to control applications, access information, and interact with services hands-free. This could include functionalities such as searching for information, managing tasks, or controlling smart home devices using only voice prompts.
