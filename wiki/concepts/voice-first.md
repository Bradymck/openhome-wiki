### definition
Voice-first refers to a design and interaction paradigm where voice is the primary mode of communication between users and technology, prioritizing spoken input and output over traditional interfaces.

### why-it-matters
Voice-first is crucial for OpenHome as it enhances accessibility and user engagement, allowing users to interact with the platform in a natural and intuitive way. This approach can facilitate more seamless communication and control of smart home devices, contributing to a more user-friendly experience.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, voice-first concepts may appear in the form of voice assistants, smart speakers, and other voice-activated interfaces that enable users to operate various applications and services hands-free.
