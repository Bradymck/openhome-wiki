## definition
Voice First refers to a design and interaction paradigm that prioritizes voice as the primary mode of communication between users and technology. It emphasizes the development of voice user interfaces (VUIs) that allow users to interact with devices and services exclusively through spoken commands.

## why-it-matters
The Voice First concept is crucial for OpenHome as it enhances user accessibility and convenience. By integrating voice interaction, OpenHome can cater to a wider audience, including those with disabilities or those who prefer hands-free operations. This approach aligns with current trends in technology, where voice assistants are becoming increasingly prevalent in everyday life.

## how-it-manifests
Voice First manifests within the OpenHome platform through various applications and services that utilize voice commands for functionality. This includes features like voice-activated control of smart devices, voice search capabilities for content, and conversational agents that assist users in navigating the platform and accessing services more intuitively.
