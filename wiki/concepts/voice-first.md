### definition
Voice First refers to a design philosophy and technological approach that prioritizes voice interaction as the primary means of user engagement with digital devices and services. It emphasizes the use of voice recognition and natural language processing to facilitate seamless and intuitive communication.

### why-it-matters
The Voice First concept is crucial for OpenHome as it aligns with the platform's goal to enhance user experiences through intuitive, hands-free interactions. By prioritizing voice commands, OpenHome can make its features more accessible and engaging, catering to a diverse range of users.

### how-it-manifests
Voice First manifests within the OpenHome platform through the integration of voice-activated functionalities. This includes the ability for users to control devices, access information, and perform tasks using natural speech. Such features enhance user experience by allowing multitasking and reducing dependency on traditional input methods.
