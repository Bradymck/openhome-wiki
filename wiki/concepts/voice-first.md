## definition
Voice-first refers to the paradigm in which voice interactions are prioritized as the primary method of user interface. In this model, users engage with technology mainly through spoken commands and queries, instead of traditional input methods like touchscreens or keyboards.

## why-it-matters
Voice-first is significant for OpenHome as it enhances accessibility and user experience. By leveraging voice interactions, OpenHome can cater to a broader audience, allowing users to interact with their environment seamlessly and intuitively. This is especially important in smart home applications, where hands-free operations can improve convenience and safety.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, voice-first capabilities are integrated into various applications that enable users to control smart devices, retrieve information, or perform tasks using voice commands. This manifests in features such as voice assistants that respond to user inquiries, manage settings, and execute commands based on natural language processing.
