## definition
Voice-first design refers to technologies and user experiences that prioritize voice interaction as the primary mode of engagement. This concept emphasizes enabling users to interact with devices, applications, and services through voice commands instead of traditional interfaces like keyboards or touchscreens.

## why-it-matters
Voice-first interactions are critical to OpenHome as they enhance accessibility and usability, allowing users to access services and information hands-free. This approach aligns with the growing trend of hands-free technology adoption, catering to a broader range of user preferences and needs.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, voice-first functionality is integrated across various applications and services. For example, users can navigate their environment, access information, and control smart home devices through voice commands. This not only simplifies interactions but also enhances the overall user experience by making technology more intuitive and responsive.
