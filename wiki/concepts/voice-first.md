## definition
Voice-first refers to a design philosophy and approach where voice interfaces are prioritized over graphical user interfaces. This involves the use of voice commands and speech recognition technologies to interact with software and devices, enabling hands-free operation and natural language processing.

## why-it-matters
Voice-first technology plays a crucial role in enhancing user interaction within the OpenHome platform. It allows for a more intuitive experience, especially in environments where traditional inputs may be challenging, such as when users are multitasking or have accessibility needs. Implementing voice-first capabilities can significantly increase user engagement and satisfaction.

## how-it-manifests
Voice-first manifests in OpenHome through various features that leverage voice commands to control applications, access information, and interact with smart devices. This can include virtual assistants, voice-activated task execution, and seamless integration with other voice-responsive technologies.
