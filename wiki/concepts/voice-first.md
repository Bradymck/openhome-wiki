## definition
Voice-first is a design and interaction paradigm that prioritizes the use of voice as the primary interface for user engagement with technology.

## why-it-matters
The voice-first approach is significant to OpenHome as it enables users to interact with the platform more intuitively and naturally, fostering accessibility and enhancing user experience. By leveraging this paradigm, OpenHome can cater to a wider audience, including those who may find traditional interfaces challenging.
