## definition
Voice-first is an approach to technology and user interface design where voice interaction is prioritized over other input methods, such as touch or keyboard. This paradigm emphasizes the use of voice commands and responses, allowing users to engage with systems primarily through spoken language.

## why-it-matters
Voice-first technology is essential to OpenHome as it enhances accessibility and user engagement. By prioritizing voice interactions, OpenHome can cater to a wider range of users, including those who may have difficulties using traditional input methods. This approach aligns with the growing trend of natural language processing and AI-driven interactions, positioning OpenHome at the forefront of user-centered design.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, voice-first capabilities appear in various features, such as voice commands for navigation, interactive voice responses for inquiries, and integration with voice assistants. These functionalities enable seamless user experiences, making tasks easier and more intuitive.
