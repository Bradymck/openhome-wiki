### definition
Voice-first design refers to the approach of prioritizing voice interactions as the primary method of engaging with technology. This involves using voice commands and responses to accomplish tasks, interact with devices, and access information.

### why-it-matters
Voice-first technology is essential for OpenHome as it enhances user accessibility and creates a more intuitive way for people to interact with digital services. By embracing voice as a primary interface, OpenHome can cater to a broader audience and facilitate hands-free control and interaction.

### how-it-manifests
In OpenHome, voice-first capabilities can be integrated into various features, such as virtual assistants or smart home management systems, where users can issue voice commands to manage their environments or access information without the need for physical interfaces.
