## definition
Voice-first refers to a user interface design philosophy that prioritizes voice interactions as the primary means of communication between users and devices. In a voice-first environment, spoken commands and responses take precedence over traditional visual interfaces.

## why-it-matters
The voice-first concept is highly relevant to OpenHome as it aligns with the increasing adoption of voice technologies, enhancing user accessibility and interaction. This approach facilitates a more natural and intuitive way for users to engage with digital environments, promoting greater inclusivity for those who may have difficulties using conventional interfaces.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, voice-first interactions manifest through various features such as voice commands for executing tasks, navigating the interface, and accessing content. These capabilities allow users to control their settings, access information, and interact with applications seamlessly through voice. The integration of voice technologies enhances the overall user experience by making it more efficient and engaging.
