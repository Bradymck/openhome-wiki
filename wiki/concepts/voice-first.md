## definition
Voice-first is a design paradigm that prioritizes voice interactions as the primary mode of user engagement with technology. It emphasizes the use of voice commands to execute tasks, retrieve information, and navigate digital environments.

## why-it-matters
Voice-first is crucial to OpenHome as it enhances user accessibility and interaction. By leveraging voice technology, OpenHome can offer a more natural and intuitive way for users to engage with their devices and services, leading to improved user experience and satisfaction.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, voice-first capabilities appear in various features, such as voice commands for home automation, voice-enabled search functions, and interactive voice response systems. These features facilitate seamless communication between users and their devices, making technology use more efficient and user-friendly.
