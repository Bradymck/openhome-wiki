## definition
Voice First refers to prioritizing voice interactions as the primary means of interfacing with technology, where voice commands and responses take precedence over traditional text-based or graphical interfaces.

## why-it-matters
Voice First is relevant to OpenHome as it enhances accessibility and user convenience, allowing users to engage with the platform in a more natural and intuitive manner. This approach aligns with the increasing trend of users preferring voice communication in their interactions with technology, making it essential for OpenHome to incorporate these capabilities.

## how-it-manifests
This concept appears in the OpenHome platform through various features that support voice commands and voice-driven actions, enabling users to control their environment, access information, and interact with applications using their voices effectively.
