### definition
Voice-first refers to a design philosophy that prioritizes voice as the primary means of interaction with technology, particularly in digital platforms and devices. This approach emphasizes creating user experiences that are optimized for voice commands, making technology more accessible and intuitive.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, the voice-first concept is crucial as it enhances user engagement through natural language interactions. By prioritizing voice as a primary interface, OpenHome aims to facilitate seamless and intuitive communication between users and the platform, thus improving accessibility and user satisfaction.

### how-it-manifests
Voice-first capabilities are integrated throughout the OpenHome platform, enabling users to perform tasks, access information, and control features through spoken commands. This includes functionalities in applications that leverage voice recognition, allowing users to interact without relying on traditional input methods such as typing or swiping.
