## definition
Local-first is a conceptual approach that emphasizes processing data primarily on the user's local device, rather than relying predominantly on cloud-based services. This paradigm enables applications to function smoothly even with limited or intermittent internet connectivity.

## why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, local-first enhances user autonomy, allowing individuals to manage and control their own data securely. This approach aligns with the growing concern over data privacy and security, enabling users to access features without constant reliance on external servers.

## how-it-manifests
Local-first principles may manifest in various functionalities across the OpenHome platform, such as offline capabilities of applications, local data storage solutions, and synchronization mechanisms that ensure data is consistent across devices without needing continuous cloud interaction.
