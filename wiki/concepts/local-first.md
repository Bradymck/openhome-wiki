## definition
Local-First is an approach to software design where applications primarily operate on local devices, emphasizing user control, performance, and offline capabilities. This strategy seeks to minimize reliance on continuous internet connectivity by storing data locally and syncing it with cloud services when necessary.

## why-it-matters
Local-First principles are crucial to OpenHome as they enhance user autonomy and privacy. By allowing users to retain control over their data and reducing dependence on remote servers, this concept aligns with OpenHome's goal of creating a more user-centric and resilient ecosystem. Local-first applications can also provide faster response times and a better user experience, especially in areas with limited internet access.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, the Local-First concept appears through features that enable users to access and interact with their applications offline. Data synchronization protocols may be implemented to ensure that changes made locally are eventually reflected in the cloud, maintaining data consistency without requiring constant internet connectivity.
