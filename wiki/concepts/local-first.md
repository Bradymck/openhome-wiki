## definition
Local First refers to a development philosophy that emphasizes the importance of local data storage and processing on devices before synchronization with cloud services or remote servers.

## why-it-matters
Local First is relevant to OpenHome as it enhances user control over their data, ensuring that personal information is stored locally until expressly needed for broader synchronization. This approach can improve data privacy, accessibility, and responsiveness of applications.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, the Local First concept is reflected through features that allow apps to function offline, storing data locally on user devices. Synchronization occurs seamlessly in the background when connectivity is re-established, allowing users to maintain a consistent experience regardless of their online status.
