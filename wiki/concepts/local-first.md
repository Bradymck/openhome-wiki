## definition
Local First refers to a design philosophy that prioritizes local data storage and processing on devices, ensuring that applications can operate effectively without a constant internet connection. This approach empowers users by enabling offline functionality and reducing reliance on cloud services.

## why-it-matters
This concept is crucial to OpenHome as it enhances user autonomy and control over data while providing robust performance in instances of limited or no internet connectivity. By emphasizing local-first principles, OpenHome can create more resilient applications that respect user privacy and reduce latency.

## how-it-manifests
Local First is integrated into various aspects of the OpenHome platform, where applications are designed to store and manage data locally on users’ devices. This ensures consistent accessibility and reliability, fostering a smoother user experience. The platform may also employ techniques like syncing and conflict resolution to maintain data integrity across local and remote instances.
