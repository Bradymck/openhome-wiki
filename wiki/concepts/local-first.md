## definition
Local-first refers to a design approach that emphasizes the execution of applications and storage of data on local devices rather than relying on a central cloud infrastructure. This concept prioritizes user autonomy and ensures that applications work seamlessly even without a constant internet connection.

## why-it-matters
Local-first is relevant to OpenHome as it enhances user privacy, reduces dependency on internet connectivity, and enables faster interactions by processing data locally. This approach is crucial for environments where connectivity can be intermittent or unreliable.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, local-first principles may manifest in various applications and tools that function effectively offline, allowing users to manage their environments and interactions without needing to connect to a central server. This ensures that user data remains accessible and secure at all times.
