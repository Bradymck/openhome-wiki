## definition
Local-first is a design philosophy that prioritizes storing and processing data locally on user devices rather than relying predominantly on cloud servers. This approach ensures that users can access and interact with their data without needing a constant internet connection.

## why-it-matters
Local-first is significant for OpenHome as it enhances user autonomy and privacy. By allowing data management to occur locally, users can maintain control over their information and reduce dependence on external servers, which can be vulnerable to outages or security breaches.

## how-it-manifests
While specific implementations of local-first have not been detailed, the principle is likely reflected in features that enable offline access, peer-to-peer data sharing, and local data synchronization within the OpenHome platform.
