### definition
Local First is an approach that emphasizes the importance of local data management and processing in applications. It prioritizes storing and manipulating data on user's local devices rather than relying solely on remote servers.

### why-it-matters
This concept is relevant to OpenHome as it enhances user autonomy, improves performance, and fosters privacy by allowing users to own and control their data directly on their devices.

### how-it-manifests
Local First manifests within the OpenHome platform by ensuring that critical functionalities can operate offline and that user interactions remain seamless regardless of connectivity. It supports scenarios where users can access and modify their data locally, with changes syncing back to the cloud when connectivity is restored.
