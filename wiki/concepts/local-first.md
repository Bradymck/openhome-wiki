## definition
Local-first refers to an approach in software design and development where applications prioritize local data storage and processing over reliance on cloud-based services. This concept emphasizes the importance of ensuring that users have access to their data offline, with seamless synchronization occurring when connectivity is available.

## why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, local-first is significant because it enhances user autonomy and data privacy. By storing data locally, users can maintain control over their information, reducing dependency on external servers and minimizing potential breaches of privacy. This approach also fosters a more resilient user experience, as applications remain functional even without internet access.

## how-it-manifests
Local-first manifests within the OpenHome platform through features that allow users to operate applications primarily from their devices without needing constant internet access. This may include synchronization capabilities that update data between local storage and the cloud when connectivity is restored, ensuring users can seamlessly transition between offline and online environments.
