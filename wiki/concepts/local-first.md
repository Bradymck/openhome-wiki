## definition
Local First refers to the approach of prioritizing local data processing and storage on user devices rather than relying solely on cloud infrastructure. This concept emphasizes the importance of offline capabilities and the ability for applications to function effectively even without an internet connection.

## why-it-matters
Local First is relevant to OpenHome as it enhances user autonomy and improves application responsiveness. By allowing data to be stored and processed locally, users can interact with their applications more seamlessly and securely. This approach also mitigates issues related to connectivity and latency, ensuring that applications remain functional and efficient.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, the Local First concept manifests through various applications and features that enable data synchronization and offline access. Users can manage their data locally, and applications can leverage caching mechanisms to provide a smooth experience regardless of internet connectivity.
