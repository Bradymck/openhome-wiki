### definition
Local First is an approach emphasizing the importance of local data and processing for applications, where operations can occur seamlessly without relying on constant internet access.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, Local First enhances user autonomy and data privacy, allowing individuals to have more control over their information. This is especially relevant in scenarios where internet connectivity may be unreliable.

### how-it-manifests
Local First principles can manifest in various ways within the OpenHome platform, such as enabling offline functionality for apps, supporting local data storage, and allowing users to interact with applications without needing a persistent internet connection.
