## definition
Local-first is a conceptual approach that prioritizes data management and processing on users' devices before synchronizing with servers or cloud environments. This approach emphasizes user autonomy, data privacy, and offline access.

## why-it-matters
Local-first is relevant to OpenHome as it provides users with greater control over their data and enhances privacy. By favoring local operations, it ensures that users remain functional even in low-connectivity situations, aligning with OpenHome's vision of empowering users through technology.

## how-it-manifests
While specific implementations within the OpenHome platform are not detailed, the local-first approach can manifest in features that allow users to manage and interact with data locally on their devices. This might include applications that store user preferences and settings locally or facilitate operations that do not require an internet connection.
