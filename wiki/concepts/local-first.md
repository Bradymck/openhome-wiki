### definition
Local First is a principle that emphasizes processing and storing data on local devices rather than relying primarily on cloud infrastructure. This approach enhances speed and privacy, allowing users to interact with their applications without constant internet connectivity.

### why-it-matters
In the context of OpenHome, Local First is crucial for empowering users to maintain control over their data and digital identities. It ensures that applications remain responsive and functional even in scenarios with limited or no internet access, fostering a more resilient ecosystem.

### how-it-manifests
The Local First concept appears in the OpenHome platform through applications designed to store user data locally while intermittently syncing with cloud services as necessary. This model enhances user experience by enabling offline capabilities and faster interactions.
