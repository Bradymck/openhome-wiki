## definition
Local first refers to an approach in software design and functionality where operations and data storage occur primarily on the user's device rather than relying heavily on cloud services. This model emphasizes resilience, speed, and user control over their data.

In the context of OpenHome, local first principles enhance user privacy and control, allowing individuals to manage their data independently. This approach supports a more personalized and responsive user experience, aligning with OpenHome's commitment to empowering users in their digital interactions.
