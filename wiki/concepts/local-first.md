### definition
Local First is a development approach that prioritizes local data storage and processing on the user's device over cloud-based solutions. This concept emphasizes enabling applications to operate smoothly even when disconnected from the internet, thus improving user experience and data privacy.

### why-it-matters
Local First is relevant to OpenHome as it aligns with the principles of user autonomy and data ownership. By allowing users to control their data locally, OpenHome fosters trust and encourages more robust engagement with its applications. This approach can enhance privacy, reduce latency, and support offline functionality, making the platform more resilient and user-friendly.

### how-it-manifests
Within OpenHome, the Local First concept manifests as features that allow users to interact with applications without requiring constant internet access. This includes capabilities for local data synchronization when connectivity is restored, ensuring a seamless transition between online and offline modes. Such functionalities are critical for users in areas with unreliable internet access.
