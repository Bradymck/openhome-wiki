## definition
Local-first is an approach to software design that emphasizes the importance of running applications and storing data directly on users' devices. This methodology prioritizes local processing and data management to minimize reliance on continuous internet connectivity.

## why-it-matters
Local-first is particularly relevant to OpenHome as it enhances user autonomy and privacy. By allowing applications to function effectively without constant internet access, it ensures that users can maintain control over their data and interactions. This approach aligns with OpenHome's commitment to user empowerment and decentralized solutions.

## how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, local-first principles are evident in applications that operate seamlessly offline, syncing data when connectivity is available. This allows users to continue working or interacting without interruptions, ensuring a smooth experience regardless of network status. Features such as local data storage and offline capabilities are critical components of the platform's design.
