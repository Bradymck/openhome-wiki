### definition
Local First refers to a design philosophy where applications primarily utilize local resources and data storage, thereby enhancing performance and user experience while reducing reliance on constant internet connectivity.

### why-it-matters
This concept is crucial to OpenHome as it promotes resilience, enabling applications to function smoothly in situations with limited or no internet access. By prioritizing local processing, OpenHome applications can provide a more responsive and reliable user experience.

### how-it-manifests
In the OpenHome platform, the Local First concept is manifested through features that facilitate data synchronization between local devices and the cloud, allowing for seamless operation even when connectivity is intermittent. This approach ensures that users can access essential functionalities without disruption.
